Spiritual protection is the practice of setting energetic boundaries — deciding, consciously, what you let into your space and what you don't. Think of it the way you'd think of locking your front door: not because you're afraid, but because a threshold is healthy.

Practitioners describe unwanted energy the way you'd describe a draft: heaviness after a tense conversation, restlessness in a new apartment, a drained feeling after crowded places. Protection work is simply the set of tools for clearing that out and keeping it out.

**Classic correspondences:** Black stones are the traditional protectors — black tourmaline, obsidian, and hematite are the three you'll find in nearly every reference. Herbs include rosemary, rue, and angelica. Salt is the oldest ward of all, used across cultures at doorways and windowsills. The evil eye symbol, worn or hung, is one of humanity's most widespread protective charms.

**Common methods:**
- **Shielding:** a short visualization where you picture light forming a boundary around you. Five slow breaths, done.
- **Wards:** small protective placements — salt across a threshold, a stone above a door, a sprig of rosemary over a window.
- **Worn protection:** a stone or amulet you carry daily, cleansed regularly (see our guide on cleansing crystals).

**Tonight:** Stand in your doorway. Sprinkle a thin line of salt across the threshold and say, quietly, "Only welcome energy enters here." Sweep it up in the morning. Simple, ancient, done.
