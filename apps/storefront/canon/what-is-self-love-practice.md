A self-love practice is a regular ritual of turning kindness inward — treating yourself with the same warmth you'd offer a dear friend. In metaphysical tradition, this is heart work: softening self-criticism, honoring your needs, and making space for your own tenderness.

**Classic correspondences:** Rose quartz is the universal stone of gentle love, traditionally associated with compassion — including compassion for oneself. Rhodonite is associated with emotional balance and forgiveness. Rose petals, rose oil, and pink candles carry the same soft signature. The heart chakra (anahata) is the energy center this work relates to.

**What a practice looks like:**
- **Morning mirror minute:** look yourself in the eye and name one true, kind thing. It feels awkward. Do it anyway.
- **Rose quartz carry:** keep the stone in a pocket as a touchstone — literally something to touch when self-criticism spikes.
- **Nourishing bath:** rose petals or rose oil in warm water, twenty unhurried minutes.
- **The letter:** write to yourself as you would to someone you love going through exactly what you're going through.

**Tonight:** Hold rose quartz — or simply place a hand over your heart. Say aloud three things you did today that were enough. Not exceptional. Enough.
