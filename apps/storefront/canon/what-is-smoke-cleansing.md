Smoke cleansing is the practice of wafting sacred smoke through a space, around objects, or around the body to clear stagnant or heavy energy. It's one of humanity's oldest purification methods — found on nearly every continent, using local plants: juniper in Europe, cedar among Pacific Northwest peoples, incense in temples across Asia.

You may know it as "smudging." That word specifically refers to the ceremonial practices of some Indigenous North American peoples, and many practitioners now prefer the broader term "smoke cleansing" for general use — a small linguistic respect worth observing.

**An important note on white sage:** White sage (*Salvia apiana*) is sacred to several Indigenous Californian peoples and has been overharvested due to commercial demand. Consider these alternatives, which are traditional in their own right:
- **Rosemary** — protection and clarity, easily grown at home
- **Cedar** — grounding and protection
- **Mugwort** — dreams and intuition
- **Lavender** — calm and gentleness
- **Palo santo** — use sparingly and buy from verifiably sustainable sources
- **Incense resins** (frankincense, copal, myrrh) — ancient temple traditions

**How to do it:** Light the bundle or stick, let it catch, then blow out the flame so it smolders. Use a feather or your hand to guide smoke around the space, keeping a fireproof dish beneath. Open a window — the old instruction is to give the cleared energy somewhere to go. Speak or think your intention: "I clear this space for peace."

**Tonight:** Tidy one room, open a window, and smoke-cleanse just the doorway you use most. Small threshold, fresh start.
